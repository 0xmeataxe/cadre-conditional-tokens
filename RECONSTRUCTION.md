# Imported source history

This repository imports the upstream history identified in PROVENANCE.md.
Commit count, parent relationships, and original author and committer dates match
upstream. The author field identifies the Cadre importer; Original-author and
Original-commit trailers retain original attribution and source commit IDs.
Cadre branding and documentation are folded into the final imported commit.
Its historical timestamp describes upstream history, not the packaging date.
